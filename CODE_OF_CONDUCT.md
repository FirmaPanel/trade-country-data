# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in FirmaPanel Trade Country Data a
respectful, inclusive, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience level, location, or role.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect and professional courtesy;
- give and receive feedback constructively;
- focus discussions on evidence, data quality, and project goals;
- handle naming, territory, and jurisdiction questions with care;
- accept reasonable moderation decisions; and
- help create an environment where people can contribute safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- insulting, demeaning, or deliberately inflammatory comments;
- unwanted sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation; or
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate professionally.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project or FirmaPanel community. Repository spaces include
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-managed channels.

## Reporting and enforcement

For routine data disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request and keep
the discussion focused on sources and methodology. To report harassment or
another sensitive concern privately, use the
[FirmaPanel contact form](https://firmapanel.com/contact) and include
“Trade Country Data conduct report” in the subject.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and protect reporters' privacy as
far as reasonably possible. Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may
edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a
participant. Retaliation for a good-faith report is not permitted.
